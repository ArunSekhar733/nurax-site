FAQ

Questions before you start

What if it doesn't work for my business?

Then you don't pay monthly. Your fee only starts after 7 days of the system working on real customers. If it isn't live in 7 days, your first month is free.

Will AI put my customers off?

You approve every message before launch, it uses your tone, and it hands over to a person the moment someone needs one. Most people just want a fast, helpful answer, and that's what they get.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We set everything up, show your team how it works in a short session, and look after it afterwards.

Do you work with my industry or country?

If customers contact you before they buy, or your team does repetitive admin, yes. We work with businesses worldwide, in your time zone.

What happens if I want to stop?

After the first 3 months, cancel anytime. Everything is in your accounts and we hand over all logins and documentation.

Why is the plan free?

Because once you see exactly what's possible and what it's worth, most businesses want it built. If you'd rather build it yourself, the plan is still yours to keep.
